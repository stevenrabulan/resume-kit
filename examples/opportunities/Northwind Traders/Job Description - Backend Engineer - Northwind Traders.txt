Backend Engineer, Order Platform
Northwind Traders
Remote (US) | Full-time

About the role

Northwind Traders moves several million orders a month for independent
retailers. The Order Platform team owns the systems that accept, validate, and
route those orders to fulfillment partners. We are looking for a backend engineer
to help us take that platform through its next order of magnitude.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role. You will own services in
production, carry a pager in a shared rotation, and have real input into
architecture decisions.

What you'll do

- Design, build, and operate high-throughput services in Go
- Own the reliability of the order pipeline end to end, including on-call
- Improve our event-driven architecture, currently built on Kafka
- Partner with the Data team to make order data available for analytics without
  a nightly batch step
- Improve the developer experience for engineers working in the platform, from
  local development through deploy
- Participate in design review and write RFCs for significant changes

What we're looking for

- 5+ years building backend services in production
- Strong Go, or strong experience in another statically typed language and a
  genuine willingness to work in Go
- Experience operating event-driven or streaming systems (Kafka, Kinesis, Pub/Sub
  or similar)
- Comfort with on-call and incident response. We care much more about how you
  handle an outage than about whether you have ever caused one
- Working knowledge of infrastructure as code (we use Terraform) and AWS
- Clear written communication. A lot of our design work happens in documents

Nice to have

- Experience with observability tooling (we use OpenTelemetry and Datadog)
- Experience improving CI or internal tooling
- Mentorship experience

What we offer

- Remote-first, with an optional office in Seattle
- 4 weeks PTO plus company holidays
- Home office and learning budgets

Northwind Traders is an equal opportunity employer.
